03

Waterproofing

Stopping leakage for good.

We create a watertight barrier over roofs, terraces, walls and foundations — protecting your building from water damage for years to come.

  • Terraces and roofs
  • Basements and walls
  • Bathrooms, kitchens and balconies
  • Water tanks and swimming pools

Methods: cementitious, liquid membrane, bituminous and polyurethane waterproofing.

Projects: Lodha New Cuffe Parade (NCP), Wadala · The Taj Mahal Palace (Swimming Pool), Mumbai · Deutsche Bank (DB House), Mumbai · Taj Falaknuma Palace, Hyderabad · Express Tower · Taj Mahal Palace Hotel, Mumbai · Taj Exotica, Goa and Andaman · Taj Connemara, Chennai · Raj Bhavan Bunker, Mumbai · Deeksha Peeth, Goa
