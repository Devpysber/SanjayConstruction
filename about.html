Who We Are

We are civil engineers in this field since 1988. Since our launch over three decades ago, our vision and focus has been to deliver high-value projects for our clients, with the emphasis on communication and attention to detail.

As an enterprise we have accomplished several prestigious projects in the construction field — industrial and new construction, restoration of old structures, machine foundation work, restoration of earthquake-affected buildings and palaces, structural steel fabrication and erection, and structural rehabilitation of buildings.

Our work includes hotels, palaces, temples, ports, factories, housing societies and schools across Mumbai, Goa, Gujarat, Kochi and Abu Dhabi.
